I also finally started researching into the Amp, and let me give a general overview of the types of amps (yes, there are types)
There are many types of amps, but I'm going to focus on 2 types: 

- Solid State (Tube) Amps: Use old-school capacitors and analog circuits to transmit the sound. Sound quality is excellent, but the capacitors operate at dangerously high voltages (300V to 500V), so it can be dangerous to build.

- Digital Amps: Use digital signal processing and computers to analyze the guitar vibrations and turn it into sound, and is very customizable. It doesn't have any high voltage capacitors, so it is safer to build, but the sound quality isn't as genuine. 

Today I researched the digital amp. One big inspiration I found is the Fender Tone Master Deluxe Reverb Amp with 9 knob controls, and a 12" 8 ohm 100W Speaker for sound.
