This project is basically about recreating that guitar as close as possible and building a digital amp to go alongside that. The research I did today was watching YouTube videos and checking out some resources to help with this project.

This video by wittworks was an incredible insight for how to build a guitar, and showing the tools and techniques to make that process easier. Through this video, I got some resources and learned a few things: Guitars take a lot of craftsmanship to create, and require a lot of specific tools and gauges to create yourself. Every single curve, spacing and angle on a guitar matters and needs to be replicated almost perfectly for a nice sound. 

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPJ0_0UJPFU

To help with that I found 2 main resources.

1. The first one is a company called Stewmac which not only specializes in guitar parts, but also the tools to build your own guitars as well. They offer measuring tools, specific sized chisels, and gauges alongside a ton of other things.
(https://www.stewmac.com)

2. The second one is a website called Scarlett Guitars which sells cutting templates and a free guide for everything a new guitar builder would need to know to pull off their own build. Super helpful.
(https://scarlettguitars.com)
